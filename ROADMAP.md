# Forked Roadmap

Plans change. Releases are named by the year and month they're expected on the
App Store (2026.12 is December 2026), and anything here can move to a later
release. Everything that is free in Forked today stays free.

Want something that isn't listed? Search the
[feature requests](https://github.com/Knownframe/forked-for-mealie/issues?q=is%3Aissue+label%3Aenhancement),
add a 👍, or [request it](https://github.com/Knownframe/forked-for-mealie/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
Released changes are in the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md).

## 2026.11: Cooking and glanceable (in testing)

Make cooking and shopping faster, with your plan in reach outside the app.

- Start Cooking shows the ingredients each step needs
- Step timers on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, several at once
- Widgets for the Home Screen and Lock Screen: what's next to cook, your plan,
  and what's left to buy
- Siri and Spotlight: ask what's next to cook, add something to Shopping, and
  find recipes by name (iOS 18 and later)
- Import a recipe from a cooking website link, checked before it's saved to
  Mealie
- Recipe pages list the tools you'll need
- Host a Meal (Forked Pro): plan a big meal for a date and your guests, scale
  every dish, follow a timeline back from serving, and cook every dish at once
  with Cook Together
- Share any meal as a menu card with photos
- Shopping suggests foods as you type
- Edit nutrition, and create or rename tags and categories
- Changes on your server arrive within minutes, and large libraries download
  faster, with time left shown
- Households show when someone joins or leaves, or changes the shared plan or
  list

## 2026.12: What people asked for

Features requested after the 2026.10 launch.

- Separate home and away server addresses
- Mark a meal cooked with a comment and photo on the Mealie timeline
- iPad layouts, and layouts for foldable iPhones
- Cookbooks: put recipes in folders, or let a smart cookbook fill itself from
  Library filters. Cookbooks stay linked to Mealie's cookbooks
- Help organizing a library: suggested tags from website data and on-device
  rules, never applied without asking
- Widgets you can configure

## 2027.1: Mealie sync and photo import

- Sync Shopping and meal plans with Mealie, both ways
- Import a recipe from a photo of a recipe card or cookbook page

## 2027.2: Cooking extras

- Apple Watch: follow steps and run timers that control your iPhone
- Hands-free cooking
- What can I make? Suggestions from a photo of your fridge
- Drag Shopping items between sections

## 2027.4: Tandoor

- Support for [Tandoor Recipes](https://tandoor.dev) as a second recipe server
- Find on My Network: when you ask, Forked looks for a Mealie or Tandoor server
  on your network. Typing an address stays available

## Questions

- **Android?** Not today. Forked is an iPhone app.
- **Other recipe servers?** Mealie today; Tandoor is next. Request others as a
  feature so the 👍 count shows demand.
