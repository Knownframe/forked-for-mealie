# Forked Roadmap

Plans change. Releases are named by the year and month they're expected on the
App Store (2026.12 is December 2026), and anything here can move to a later
release. Everything that is free in Forked today stays free.

Want something that isn't listed? Search the
[feature requests](https://github.com/Knownframe/forked-for-mealie/issues?q=is%3Aissue+label%3Aenhancement),
add a 👍, or [request it](https://github.com/Knownframe/forked-for-mealie/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
Released changes are in the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md), and problems we
know about are in [known issues](KNOWN_ISSUES.md).

## 2026.12: What people asked for

Waiting for App Review. Details will be in the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md) when
it's out.

- Separate home and away server addresses
- Cookbooks: put recipes in folders, or let a smart cookbook fill itself from
  Library filters. Cookbooks stay linked to Mealie's cookbooks
- Sync Shopping with a Mealie shopping list, both ways, free
- Mark a meal cooked with a comment and photo on the Mealie timeline
- iPad layouts, and layouts for foldable iPhones
- Cooking timers that ring like the Clock app's until you stop them, even in
  silent mode (iOS 26)
- Fixes from the [known issues](KNOWN_ISSUES.md)

## 2026.12.1: Large libraries and fixes

A follow-up to 2026.12.0, planned for mid-December.

- A large library finishes its first download instead of stopping partway,
  and Force Full Sync no longer takes an hour on a big library
- Edit a recipe's tools in the recipe editor
- Recipe Settings (Public, comments, nutrition, files, Locked) when you create
  a recipe, not only when you edit one
- Plan's Hosting tab stays smooth while a sync runs

## 2027.1: Photo import

- Sync meal plans with Mealie, both ways, free
- Import a recipe from a photo of a recipe card or cookbook page (Forked Pro)
- Household members see a hosted meal in full, with its serving time, guests,
  and timeline
- Move a Household's plan and Shopping to Mealie, only if you choose to
- Plan entries that aren't recipes, like Leftovers or Eat Out
- Check off Shopping items from a widget
- A list view in Library, to see more recipes at once
- More than one photo for a recipe
- Refresh a recipe from its website and fix missing details
- More holidays and seasons in Quick Picks
- Grams and milliliters on demand (Forked Pro)
- Saved filters (Forked Pro)
- Share a recipe as a link
- Tap a time in a step to start a timer

## 2027.2: Translations and organizing

- Forked in French, with allergy and diet matching for French ingredient
  names
- Help organizing a library: suggested tags from website data and on-device
  rules, never applied without asking
- Widgets you can configure
- Side suggestions for a main dish, and mains for a side (Forked Pro)
- A redesigned Profile page
- Drag Shopping items between sections (iOS 27)

## 2027.3: Import and backup

- Import your Paprika and Mela recipes into Mealie
- Back up and restore Forked's plans, Plan History, Shopping, and profiles,
  free
- Sign in with SSO for Mealie servers behind Authentik, Authelia, and similar
- Photos on individual steps (Forked Pro)

## 2027.10: Holiday season

- "Last Thanksgiving you made…" from your hosted meals (Forked Pro)
- Scale a baking recipe to the number of pieces you need
- Share a recipe as a card for cookie exchanges

## Later

No release yet.

- Apple Watch: follow steps and run timers that control your iPhone
- Hands-free cooking
- What can I make? Suggestions from a photo of your fridge
- Support for [Tandoor Recipes](https://tandoor.dev) as a second recipe server
- Find on My Network: when you ask, Forked looks for a Mealie or Tandoor server
  on your network. Typing an address stays available

## Questions

- **Android?** Not today. Forked is an iPhone app, with iPad in 2026.12.
- **Other recipe servers?** Mealie today. Tandoor is planned for later. Request
  others as a feature so the 👍 count shows demand.
